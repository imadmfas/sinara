## The teaching experience

Teach useful tasks as well as prompting: choose when an AI assistant is useful, describe the job, supply relevant material, specify constraints, inspect its output, verify a claim and choose the next question. Build the signature تجربة «غيّر كلمة، شوف الفرق». The learner predicts what will change, alters exactly one meaningful instruction, compares two worked outputs, and explains which one serves their purpose. Highlight the causal edit with text as well as motion. Give evidence tied to a rubric. A short, sufficient request can succeed. Reserve numeric scoring for tasks with an objective calculation or a published clear rubric. Never reward length or fancy wording.

Implement differentiated tracks: 4–6 caregiver-led pictures and spoken choices, 7–9 concrete household/school tasks, 10–12 guided testing and verification, 13–17 independent projects, 18–22 university and vocational practice, 23+ professional and daily life. Add optional beginner mode, large type, paced instructions and examples for older adults or users with limited reading skills. Expertise and age are separate settings. Preserve local age-band storage and a shared-device reset. In child paths use curated local content and a caregiver route to resources within age scope; keep open provider chat, raw personal inputs and adult-only examples out of those paths.

Make six distinct demonstration lessons: a preschooler changes the story setting; a school pupil requests a hint and then solves independently; a senior finds a phone photo; a shopkeeper compares two quantities priced in Iraqi dinars; a university researcher detects a fabricated attribution in an explicitly fictional example and asks for the supplied text; a homeowner revises a conceptual schedule when stair access is added. Each includes a near-miss, a feedback rule, a second scenario with the answer withheld and an observable completion condition. Include separate practice for text, images, tables, voice/visual briefs and supplied documents. Teach users how to distinguish a tool limitation, missing data and an incorrect answer.

## Deliver an honest library of thousands

It contains 120 practical situations distributed across 12 collections, each expressed as 20 different work requests, giving 2,400 filled, copy-ready variants. These are generated instructional variants with fictional inputs. They are not 2,400 independently authored cases or human-reviewed expert answers. Preserve IDs and provenance. The input collections are editorial shelves; map these to the site's existing fields and branches with an explicit crosswalk. Some entries belong on multiple shelves. Do not drop existing branches, infer reading age from a professional title or expose the entire dataset to preschool learners.

Import the supplied records into the actual application. Inspect all entries with automated checks, particularly whether a mode is appropriate to the scenario. Quarantine incoherent or out-of-scope pairs. Give each record an editorial status (imported, validated, reviewed, held), origin, suitable age bands, difficulty, required data and model/tool capability. Use “reviewed” only after a recorded editorial review. Ship the functional catalogue from day one and show accurate counts derived from the loaded manifest.
